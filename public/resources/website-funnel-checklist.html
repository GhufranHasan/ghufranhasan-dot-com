10-Point Website Funnel Checklist

Use this to check whether your website continues the conversation after someone discovers you on LinkedIn.

  1. 1. One clear promiseYour hero should say who you help, what outcome you create, and why it matters.
  2. 2. LinkedIn message matchThe website headline should continue the same promise visitors saw on your profile.
  3. 3. One primary CTAVisitors should not have to choose between too many next steps.
  4. 4. Proof before pitchShow credibility, experience, process, or examples before asking for a call.
  5. 5. Problem clarityMake the visitor feel understood before explaining your service.
  6. 6. Offer clarityExplain what you do in plain language, not vague service labels.
  7. 7. Decision pathThe page should move from problem to solution to proof to process to CTA.
  8. 8. Objection handlingAnswer common doubts about budget, testimonials, timing, and fit.
  9. 9. Friction-free contactCalendly, email, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp should be easy to find and use.
  10. 10. Mobile trust checkRead the page on mobile and make sure the CTA, proof, and offer are visible without confusion.
